Lesson 5: Responsive Images, Figures & Media Formats

Master image accessibility (alt attributes), modern WebP/AVIF formats, figures, captions, and responsive pictures.

1. Core Image Syntax (<img>) & Accessibility

Always specify the alt attribute for screen-readers and fallback SEO, as well as width, height, and loading="lazy" for performance.

<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80" 
     alt="Satellite view of planet Earth at night" 
     width="600" 
     height="350" 
     loading="lazy">

2. Semantic <figure> & <figcaption>

The <figure> element encapsulates self-contained photographic or diagrammatic media alongside a descriptive <figcaption>.
