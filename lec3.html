Lesson 3: Horizontal Rules, Quotations & Citations

Thematic breaks, block quotations, inline quotes, and citation tags.

1. Thematic Break (<hr>)

In HTML5, the <hr> element represents a thematic break between paragraph-level elements (e.g. a change of scene or topic shift).
